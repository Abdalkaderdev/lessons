Paper trading means executing your strategy with fake money on a real market feed. Every beginner should do it for **at least 3 to 6 months** before risking a single real dollar. The goal is simple: prove your strategy works on paper before it has the chance to wipe out your savings.

---

## Why paper trading is non-negotiable

Most retail traders lose money. Studies from regulators in the US, Europe, and Brazil consistently show that **70 to 90 percent of retail traders are not profitable** over a year. Paper trading is the cheapest way to find out whether you are in the unlucky majority before you fund a live account.

It also separates "I read about a strategy" from "I can actually execute a strategy under pressure." Those are completely different skills.

## What to track during paper trading

A paper-trade run only counts if you log it. Use a journal (Notion, Google Sheets, or the AI-assisted journal from the Trading Psychology module).

| Metric | Target before going live |
|---|---|
| Total trades | At least 50 |
| Win rate | Whatever your strategy expects, consistently |
| Average R:R (reward-to-risk) | 1.5:1 or better |
| Max drawdown | Under 20% of paper account |
| Rule violations | Trending toward zero |
| Months of consistent results | 3 minimum, 6 ideal |

**Warning:** A 10-trade winning streak proves nothing. Variance is huge over small samples. 50 trades is the absolute minimum.

---

## How to know when you are ready

You are ready for real money only when **all** of these are true:

- You have logged 50+ paper trades across at least 3 months
- Your strategy has a positive expectancy (average R x win rate > average loss x loss rate)
- You followed your own rules on 90%+ of trades
- You can explain, in writing, why each trade was a valid setup
- Your max drawdown did not scare you out of the strategy

If any of these fail, **keep paper trading**. There is no rush. The market will still be there next year.

## Use AI to...

Review your paper-trading journal weekly. Paste your trade log into Claude or ChatGPT with this prompt:

```text
You are my trading coach. Below is my paper trading journal from the last 7 days.
For each trade I have: entry, exit, R:R planned, R:R achieved, setup type, and a note.

1. Calculate my win rate, average R, and expectancy for the week.
2. Identify the 2 setup types that are working and the 2 that are losing money.
3. Flag any trades where I broke my own rules (oversized, no stop, revenge trade, FOMO entry).
4. Give me one specific behavioral change for next week.

Be blunt. Do not flatter me.

[paste your trades here]
```

Run this every Sunday. Patterns show up fast.
