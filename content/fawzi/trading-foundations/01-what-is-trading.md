# What Is Trading?

Trading is the act of **buying something with the intention of selling it later for a different price**. If you buy a stock at $100 and sell it at $110, you made $10. That's trading in its simplest form — no charts, no jargon, just price movement and timing.

---

## Trading vs. Investing

People mix these up constantly. They're related, but the **time horizon and intent** make them different jobs.

| Aspect | Trading | Investing |
|---|---|---|
| Time horizon | Seconds to weeks | Years to decades |
| Goal | Profit from price changes | Build wealth from ownership |
| Cares about | Price action, momentum | Business fundamentals, dividends |
| Decision speed | Fast, frequent | Slow, rare |
| Example | Buy AAPL Monday, sell Friday | Buy AAPL, hold 10 years |

**Bottom line:** Investors own things. Traders rent them.

---

## Buy Low, Sell High (and the other direction)

The headline rule is **buy low, sell high**. But traders can also profit when prices fall — that's called **shorting**. You borrow an asset, sell it now at a high price, buy it back later at a lower price, and pocket the difference.

So really, traders make money from **price movement in either direction**, as long as they predict it correctly.

The hard part is not the formula. It's that nobody rings a bell at the top or the bottom. You're making decisions with incomplete information, and you'll be wrong often. **Good traders don't predict perfectly — they manage being wrong.**

---

## Who Else Is in the Market?

When you click "buy," you're not playing alone. There are three main groups on the other side of your trade:

- **Retail traders** — individuals like you, trading from a phone or laptop. Usually the smallest accounts and the least information.
- **Institutions** — hedge funds, banks, pension funds, mutual funds. They move millions or billions per trade. They have research teams, faster data, and lawyers.
- **Market makers** — firms (like Citadel, Virtu) whose job is to always quote a buy price and a sell price. They profit from the tiny gap between them. Without market makers, you couldn't trade instantly.

You're a small fish. That's fine — small fish can move faster than whales. But never forget who you're swimming with.
