# Swing Trading

Swing trading holds positions from a few days to a few weeks, aiming to capture a single "swing" in price. You make decisions in the evening, place orders, and let the market work while you live your life. **This is the most realistic style for people with a regular job.**

---

## Why Swing Trading Suits Working People

| Factor | Day Trading | Swing Trading |
|---|---|---|
| Screen time | 6+ hours/day | 30-60 min/day |
| PDT rule ($25k) | Applies | Does not apply |
| Commission/slippage drag | High | Low |
| Emotional pressure | Extreme | Moderate |
| Compatible with a job | No | Yes |
| Realistic for beginners | No | Yes |

You can analyze charts at night, set entry orders and stops with your broker, and check in once during your lunch break. The market does the work.

---

## Core Swing Setups

### Trend Pullback
The strongest, simplest setup for beginners.

- Identify a stock in a clear uptrend (price above rising 50-day moving average)
- Wait for a pullback to the 20-day MA or a prior support level
- Enter when price shows reversal (bullish engulfing candle, hammer, etc.)
- Stop below the pullback low
- Target the prior swing high or a measured move

### Range Breakout
- Stock consolidates in a tight range for 2-6 weeks (a "base")
- Volume contracts during the base
- Enter on a breakout above the range on expanding volume
- Stop below the breakout level or mid-range
- Target = range height projected upward

### Earnings Drift
- Stock gaps up on a strong earnings report
- Often drifts higher for days/weeks after
- Higher risk because of analyst upgrades, short squeezes, news reversals

## Position Sizing

The single most important formula in trading:

```
Position Size = (Account Risk $) / (Entry - Stop)
```

Example: $20,000 account, risk 1% = $200 per trade.
- Entry: $50
- Stop: $48
- Risk per share: $2
- Position size: $200 / $2 = **100 shares**

**Never skip this calculation.** Position sizing is what separates traders from gamblers.

---

## Typical Risk/Reward

A realistic swing trade aims for:

- **Risk:Reward of 1:2 or better** (risk $1 to make $2+)
- **Win rate around 40-50%** (yes, you lose more often than you win)
- **Holding period: 3-15 trading days**

At 45% win rate and 1:2 R:R, your expectancy per trade is positive even though you lose almost half the time. **Most beginners blow up because they take 1:1 trades with a 50% win rate and add commissions/slippage on top.**
