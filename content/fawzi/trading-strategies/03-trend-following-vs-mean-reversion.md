# Trend Following vs Mean Reversion

Almost every trading strategy belongs to one of two philosophies: **trend following** (price in motion stays in motion) or **mean reversion** (price stretched far from average returns to it). They are opposite worldviews, and the personality that thrives in one usually struggles with the other.

---

## Trend Following

**Core bet:** Once a trend is established, it persists longer than people expect.

- Buy strength, sell weakness
- Examples: breakout systems, moving average crossovers, turtle trading, Donchian channels
- Famous practitioners: Richard Dennis, Ed Seykota, Bill Dunn, Man AHL

### What it feels like

- **Lots of small losses.** Most breakouts fail.
- **Occasional huge wins** that pay for all the losses and then some.
- Win rate often **30-40%**, but average winner is 3-5x average loser.
- Long psychological droughts where nothing works for months.

---

## Mean Reversion

**Core bet:** Price extremes are temporary; price returns to its average.

- Buy weakness, sell strength
- Examples: RSI(2) oversold bounces, Bollinger Band fades, pairs trading, sell-the-rip in ranges
- Famous practitioners: Larry Connors, statistical arbitrage desks

### What it feels like

- **Lots of small wins.** You feel like a genius.
- **Occasional huge loss** when the trend doesn't reverse and you catch a falling knife.
- Win rate often **65-75%**, but a few bad trades can wipe out months of gains.
- Tempting to remove stops "just this once" — which is how accounts die.

---

## Side-by-Side

| Dimension | Trend Following | Mean Reversion |
|---|---|---|
| Buys | Strength / breakouts | Weakness / pullbacks |
| Win rate | 30-40% | 65-75% |
| Avg win vs loss | Win >> Loss | Win < Loss |
| Worst case | Long drought, slow bleed | One trade blows up the account |
| Best market | Trending, volatile | Range-bound, low volatility |
| Worst market | Choppy ranges | Strong trends |
| Emotional load | Patience, accepting losses | Discipline to take stops |
| Holding period | Weeks to months | Hours to days |

---

## Which One Suits Which Person

**Trend following suits you if:**
- You can stomach being wrong 6 out of 10 times
- You're patient and don't need constant action
- You can let winners run without taking profits early
- You won't abandon the system after a 6-month drawdown

**Mean reversion suits you if:**
- You enjoy frequent feedback and small wins
- You are **ruthless about stop losses** (this is non-negotiable)
- You like quantitative analysis and statistical edges
- You can resist averaging down when a trade goes against you

---

## The Trap

The most common beginner mistake is **switching styles after a losing streak**. Trend follower loses 5 in a row, sees a mean-reversion guru, switches. Loses 3 there, sees a breakout guru, switches back. This is how accounts get destroyed.

**Pick one. Trade it for at least 50 trades. Then decide if it suits you.**
