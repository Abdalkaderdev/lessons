# Day Trading Basics

Day trading means opening and closing positions within the same trading session — you never hold overnight. The goal is to profit from small intraday moves, often using leverage. **It looks exciting on YouTube. In reality, most retail day traders lose money.**

---

## The Brutal Truth

Multiple academic studies (Brazil, Taiwan, US) put the share of retail day traders who are net profitable after one year at roughly **1-3%**. The rest break even or lose. The ones who survive treat it as a full-time profession with strict risk rules — not a side hustle.

If you are not prepared to treat this like a job, swing trading (next lesson) is a more realistic path.

---

## The Pattern Day Trader (PDT) Rule

If you trade US stocks through a US broker:

- 4 or more day trades in 5 business days flags you as a "Pattern Day Trader"
- You must maintain **$25,000 minimum equity** in a margin account
- Below $25k, your account gets restricted to 3 day trades per 5 days

**Workarounds people use** (each has tradeoffs):

| Workaround | Tradeoff |
|---|---|
| Use a non-US broker | Currency risk, weaker regulation, harder to withdraw |
| Trade futures (e.g. MES) | Different product, leverage cuts both ways |
| Trade forex / crypto | 24/7 markets, but spreads and weekend gaps |
| Cash account, no margin | T+1 settlement limits round trips |

## Common Day Trading Setups

These are *patterns*, not magic. Each has a failure rate.

### Opening Range Breakout (ORB)
- Mark the high and low of the first 15-30 minutes
- Enter when price breaks above the high (long) or below the low (short)
- Stop loss on the opposite side of the range

### VWAP Bounce
- VWAP = Volume Weighted Average Price (institutional benchmark)
- In an uptrend, price often pulls back to VWAP and bounces
- Enter on the bounce, stop below VWAP

### Gap and Go
- Stock gaps up on news, holds the gap, breaks the premarket high
- High failure rate without volume confirmation

---

## Time Requirements

Day trading is a job. Expect:

- **Pre-market prep:** 60-90 minutes (news, gappers, levels)
- **Live trading:** Full focus during the first 1-2 hours and last hour
- **Review:** 30-60 minutes journaling every trade

If you have a 9-to-5, you cannot day trade US markets. **Don't pretend otherwise.**
