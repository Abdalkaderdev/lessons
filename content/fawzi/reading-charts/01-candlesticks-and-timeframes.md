# Candlesticks and Timeframes

A candlestick is a small picture of what price did during a chosen period — one minute, one hour, one day. Each candle tells you four things: where price opened, where it closed, and the highest and lowest points in between. Once you can read one candle, you can read a whole chart.

---

## Anatomy of a Candle

Every candle has two parts:

- **Body** — the fat rectangle between the open and close
- **Wicks (shadows)** — the thin lines above and below the body, marking the high and low

| Part | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| Open | Price at the start of the period |
| Close | Price at the end of the period |
| High | Highest price reached |
| Low | Lowest price reached |
| Body | Distance between open and close |
| Wick | Distance from body to high/low |

**Bullish candle** (usually green): close is **higher** than open — buyers won.
**Bearish candle** (usually red): close is **lower** than open — sellers won.

---

## Common Patterns to Recognize

These three show up everywhere. Memorize them.

| Pattern | What it looks like | What it usually means |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Doji** | Tiny body, long wicks both sides | Indecision — buyers and sellers tied |
| **Hammer** | Small body on top, long wick below | Possible bottom — sellers tried, buyers pushed back |
| **Engulfing** | Big candle that fully covers the previous one | Strong reversal — the new side is in control |

A pattern alone is not a signal. It matters more **where** it appears (at a key level) than that it appears at all.

---

## Timeframes: Which One to Use

Each candle represents one chunk of time. You pick the chunk.

| Timeframe | One candle = | Best for |
|-----------|--------------|----------|
| 1m / 5m | 1 or 5 minutes | Scalping, very short trades — noisy |
| 15m / 1h | 15 min / 1 hour | Intraday entries and exits |
| 4h | 4 hours | Swing trades, multi-day setups |
| 1D (daily) | 1 day | The standard for most traders |
| 1W (weekly) | 1 week | Big-picture trend, long-term view |

**Rule of thumb for beginners:** start on the **daily**. Zoom out to the weekly to see the trend. Zoom in to 1h only when you are ready to enter.
