# Trends and Patterns

The market is always doing one of three things: going up, going down, or going sideways. Spotting which one you are in — before you trade — is more valuable than any pattern. Patterns just help you guess **what comes next** once you know the trend.

---

## The Three Market States

| State | What it looks like | What to do |
|-------|-------------------|------------|
| **Uptrend** | Price making higher highs and higher lows | Buy dips, ride the trend |
| **Downtrend** | Lower highs and lower lows | Sell rallies, or stay out |
| **Sideways / Range** | Price bouncing between a ceiling and a floor | Buy at the floor, sell at the ceiling — or wait |

**Rule:** trade **with** the trend on higher timeframes. Fighting it is how beginners lose money.

---

## Higher Highs, Higher Lows

This is the simplest definition of a trend, and the only one you really need.

- **Uptrend** = each peak is higher than the last peak, AND each dip is higher than the last dip.
- **Downtrend** = each peak is lower, AND each dip is lower.
- **Range** = peaks and dips bounce around the same area.

The moment a trend **stops making higher highs** — pay attention. That is often the earliest sign the trend is dying.

---

## Reversal Patterns

These suggest the current trend is ending.

| Pattern | Shape | Meaning |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Head and Shoulders** | Three peaks, middle one highest | Top of an uptrend — reversal down |
| **Inverse H&S** | Three valleys, middle one lowest | Bottom of a downtrend — reversal up |
| **Double Top** | Two peaks at the same level (M shape) | Resistance held twice — likely drop |
| **Double Bottom** | Two valleys at the same level (W shape) | Support held twice — likely rise |

Reversal patterns are only valid **after** price breaks the "neckline" — the level connecting the lows (or highs) of the pattern. Until that breaks, the pattern is just a shape.

---

## Continuation Patterns

These suggest the current trend is **pausing** before continuing.

| Pattern | Shape | Usually leads to |
|---------|-------|------------------|
| **Ascending Triangle** | Flat top, rising bottom | Breakout up |
| **Descending Triangle** | Flat bottom, falling top | Breakdown down |
| **Symmetrical Triangle** | Both sides squeezing in | Breaks in direction of prior trend |
| **Bull Flag** | Sharp rise, then a small downward channel | Continuation up |
| **Bear Flag** | Sharp drop, then a small upward channel | Continuation down |
| **Rising Wedge** | Both lines slope up, narrowing | Often breaks **down** |
| **Falling Wedge** | Both lines slope down, narrowing | Often breaks **up** |

**Flags after a strong move** are some of the cleanest setups in trading. Look for them after a big candle.

---

## What Beginners Get Wrong

- Seeing patterns that are not there (your brain finds shapes in noise)
- Trading the pattern **before** it confirms (wait for the breakout close)
- Ignoring the **bigger timeframe** trend — a bull flag on 5m means nothing if the daily is dumping
